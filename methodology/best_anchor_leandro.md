# Best Anchor Stocks (Leandro)

## Core edge
Build a portfolio of **"anchor stocks"** — businesses so durable you'd be comfortable holding 10+
years — and let time + compounding do the work.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **"Easy to hold 10+ years" filter.** If you wouldn't be comfortable owning it through a multi-
  year drawdown, it's not an anchor.
- **Reverse-DCF first.** Start from what the price implies before any narrative.
- **Volatility treated as real risk** for an individual position (not just opportunity) — sizing
  accounts for how much pain the position can inflict.
- **Sell only on a broken thesis.** Low turnover; conviction over trading.

## How agents use it
- **Contrarian scout** uses the "great business, temporarily out of favor" lens.
- **Valuation agent** applies reverse-DCF-first discipline.
- **Risk/PM** weighs single-name volatility in sizing.
