# Compounding Quality (Pieter Slegers)

## Core edge
Own a tight universe of **high-quality compounders** — businesses with high, sustainable ROIC and
a long reinvestment runway — bought at a reasonable price and held for years.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Quality Score /10** from a structured rubric (~13 qualitative criteria + ~15 quantitative
  metrics): ROIC trend, margins, balance-sheet strength, growth durability, insider ownership,
  capital allocation, etc. This is the basis for the system's Stage-2 `quality_score`.
- **ROIC as north star.** Demand high *and sustainable* returns on capital; watch the trend, not
  just the level.
- **Owner-operator ≥ ~10% insider ownership.** Skin in the game is a first-class variable.
- **Reverse-DCF with an expected-return hurdle** (≥~10%). If the implied growth needed to clear
  the hurdle is unrealistic, pass.
- **Narrow, curated universe** (~150 names) — quality first, then wait for price.

## How agents use it
- **Quality-compounder scout** screens for the ROIC + reinvestment + reasonable-price profile.
- **Moat/Quality agent** computes the Quality Score /10 using this rubric.
- **Valuation agent** applies the ≥10% expected-return hurdle alongside MBI's IRR floor.
