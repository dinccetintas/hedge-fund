# Doomberg

## Core edge
Top-down **energy and commodities macro** — a lens on the physical economy (power, fuels,
materials, grid) that often front-runs equity themes.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Energy-first macro.** Follow the molecules and electrons: power costs, fuel supply chains,
  commodity cycles drive second-order equity beneficiaries.
- **Physical-world constraints** as a reality check on narratives (e.g. an "AI" theme is also a
  power/cooling/grid theme).

## How agents use it
- **Thematic scout** uses this as a macro input to spot energy/commodity-driven secular themes
  and their second-order beneficiaries; feeds the **Themes & Outlook** view.
