# From 0 to 1 (Giuliano Mana)

*Terry-Smith disciple ("buy good companies, don't overpay, do nothing").*

## Core edge
The **"reinvestment moat"**: the multibagger math is dominated by a company's ability to redeploy
cash at high incremental returns for many years — returns come from compounding, not re-rating.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Reinvestment moat.** It's not enough to have a high ROIC today; the question is whether the
  business can keep investing *incremental* capital at high returns for a decade+.
- **Returns on incremental capital (ROIIC)** over headline ROIC.
- **Returns from compounding, not multiple expansion.** Don't rely on the market re-rating you;
  underwrite the business's own engine.

## How agents use it
- **Quality-compounder scout** and **Moat/Quality agent** assess the reinvestment runway and
  returns on *incremental* capital explicitly (`QualityAssessment.reinvestment_runway`).
