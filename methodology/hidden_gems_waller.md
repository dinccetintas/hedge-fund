# Hidden Gems / Plural (Chris Waller)

*Notable for a real, externally-visible fund record (not just a newsletter).*

## Core edge
**Small-cap "sleuthing"** — find niche market leaders with high returns on capital, trading
cheaply because they're under-followed, and do the legwork others won't.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Niche-leader profile:** dominant in a small market, ~15–20% ROIC, trading ~10–13× earnings,
  little/no sell-side coverage.
- **Deep scuttlebutt** — on the order of ~20 expert/customer/competitor calls before buying.
- **Concentration with patience:** ~7–8 names, 3–5 year holds.
- Under-coverage is the edge: fewer eyes → more mispricing.

## How agents use it
- **Hidden-gem small-cap scout** screens for niche leaders at 15–20% ROIC / low multiple / low
  coverage, then layers Bigdata.com qualitative checks to approximate the scuttlebutt step.
