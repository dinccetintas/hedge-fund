# Oguz Erkan — Capitalist Letters

*The user's favorite. Closest articulation of the asymmetric, edge-driven style this system clones.*

## Core edge
Returns come from **asymmetry** ("heads we win, tails we don't lose much") combined with an
**edge that is analytical × behavioral**, not informational. The mispricing is *time-arbitrage*:
being willing to be right slowly, over years the crowd won't wait.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Asymmetry framing.** Before sizing upside, define downside and what's already priced in. A
  position only earns capital if upside ≫ downside.
- **Edge = analytical × behavioral.** Informational edge is dead. Ask explicitly: *what do we
  understand or tolerate that the market doesn't?* If neither, there is no edge — pass.
- **5-step competitive-advantage (moat) analysis** — network effects, switching costs, cost
  advantage, intangibles/brand, scale. Score each on **strength and direction** (widening /
  stable / eroding). Durability matters more than current width.
- **Proven-model-in-a-new-market pattern** ("Amazon-of-X"): a de-risked business model entering
  an underpenetrated geography/vertical. The model risk is already retired; the bet is execution
  + TAM.
- **Reverse-DCF + margin of safety.** Back out what the price implies; demand a real MoS.
- **Start small; add on validation.** Don't treat your assumptions as the base case. Add only
  when the thesis proves out *and the price still lags*.
- **Two pre-armed sell rules:** (a) upside capped relative to position → trim and reallocate to
  better optionality (the "InPost" rule); (b) the thesis cracks → exit (the "Hims" rule).

## How agents use it
- **Thematic & transplant scouts** apply the asymmetry + proven-model lens.
- **Moat/Quality agent** runs the 5-step analysis with direction scoring.
- **Edge Gate** is essentially Oguz's analytical×behavioral + time-arbitrage test.
- **Risk/PM** uses start-small sizing and pre-arms the two sell rules.
