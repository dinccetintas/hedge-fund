# Speedwell Research

## Core edge
**Extreme-depth primary research** — understand a business better than almost anyone, "like a
camera slowly focusing its lens," before forming a valuation view.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Primary-source depth.** Go past the 10-K: channel checks, industry primary sources,
  competitor and customer perspectives.
- **Counterfactual interrogation of management claims.** Don't accept the narrative — ask what
  would have to be true, and what evidence would contradict it.
- **Reverse-DCF anchored in deep operating understanding** rather than spreadsheet extrapolation.

## How agents use it
- **Moat/Quality agent** uses the counterfactual lens (and Bigdata.com transcripts/filings) to
  pressure-test the durability story.
- **Valuation agent** grounds the reverse-DCF in business reality, not trend-fitting.
