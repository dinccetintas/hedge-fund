# The Wolf of Harcourt Street

## Core edge
**GARP (growth at a reasonable price), Lynch-style**, applied to modern platform/compounder
businesses, with full transparency on the numbers.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Inverse/reverse-DCF** to sanity-check what the multiple is paying for.
- **Lynch lens:** understandable businesses, reasonable PEG, durable growth.
- **Full transparency:** show the model, the assumptions, and the track record.

## How agents use it
- **Valuation agent** uses inverse-DCF as a cross-check and prefers reasonable PEG for
  growth names.
