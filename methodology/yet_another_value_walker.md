# Yet Another Value (Andrew Walker)

## Core edge
**Special situations** and event-driven value: spinoffs, forced selling, busted IPOs/SPACs, and
catalysts the market misprices for structural (non-fundamental) reasons.

## Distinctive techniques (encode these)
- **Forced selling as a *buy* signal.** Index deletions, spin-related dumping, and mandate-driven
  selling create non-economic sellers — the cleanest source of mispricing.
- **Spinoffs:** "buy the better business in the spin." Study which entity gets the quality assets
  and which inherits the stigma/leverage.
- **Downside-first, catalyst-aware.** Define the floor; identify the specific event that closes
  the gap and roughly when.
- Healthy skepticism of management spin and promotional narratives.

## How agents use it
- **Special-situations scout** sources spinoffs, forced-selling events, busted IPOs/SPACs.
- **Red Team** stress-tests management claims and the catalyst path.
